No changeLiferay and Marketplace apps keep their names; the UI shows where they come from

Cost

Changing names costs words. Not changing them costs clarity.

If we change

UI labelsLanguage keys, no new screens

Docs and HelpProduct names in titles

Marketing reviewExternal names and timing

If we don’t

More patternsEvery new product picks its own

Harder to sellThe suite is harder to explain

Harder to designThe new Applications Home inherits the mess

Questions we expect

1Doesn’t Gartner expect category names?It asks for one integrated product under one name, and readable portfolios. Category words go in descriptions.
2What happens to the Global Menu?Nothing yet. It will read “Content”; navigation is the next workstream.
3Why rename DXP?It names the suite, not one product. Gartner evaluates that umbrella name, so Marketing decides if and when it changes.
