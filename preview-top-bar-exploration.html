Preview Top Bar Exploration
LPD-106078

Full-saturation primary on a persistent, full-width bar reads as loud rather than as system chrome. That is the problem these options are measured against. The bar also needs a signal icon that nothing else in the product already claims.

The hue itself stays blue. The Launches proof of concept already codes the launch path in blue throughout, against orange for publications. This bar exists to say “you are viewing a launch’s pinned versions”, so staying blue keeps that vocabulary instead of inventing a new color meaning just for preview. What is open is how much of that blue to use.

1 · Blue weight

Version: 1 Site: Bootcamp

Vivid

What ships today. Clears AA at 5.13:1, but full saturation across the whole width is what makes the bar feel like a banner instead of a system signal. Baseline, not the recommendation.

--color-primary
Preview Launch
Version: 1 Site: Bootcamp

Deepest

Recommendation. Two steps down the same ramp, still unmistakably blue, still inside --color-primary so it keeps the same light/dark contract as every other primary-filled control. Contrast improves to 7.09:1, and it is the darkest step the ramp has before leaving primary for a chart token.

--color-primary-d2
