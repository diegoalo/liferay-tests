Input Text AI Variant

The five AI states of the text input, side by side in light and dark. Everything here is painted by the real Lexicon stylesheet taken out of DXP 7.4.13-u152, so the colours are the ones the product renders, not a mock-up.

Only Default and Focused can be reached in the product today. Working waits on the AI service being connected, and neither Result state is triggered from anywhere yet. This page is the only place all five can be seen together, which is why it exists.
